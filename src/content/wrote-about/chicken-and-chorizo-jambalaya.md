A little bit of spice that keeps the kids happy.

<!-- excerpt -->

| Serves | Cook Time |
| --- | --- |
| 2–3 | 40 min |

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amt | Prepped |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chicken Breast | 500g | Cubed |
| Onion | 2 large | Diced |
| Red Bell Pepper | 1 large | Sliced |
| Chorizo | 75g | Diced |
| Rice | 250g | |
| Chopped Tomatoes | 1 can | |
| Chicken Stock | 1 cube | Dissolved in 350ml boiling water |
| Cajun Seasoning | 1 tbsp | |
| Olive Oil | 4 tbsp | |

## Steps

1. Put 2 tbsp **Olive Oil** and cubed **Chicken Breast** in a pot and heat until the chicken is cooked all the way through.
2. Remove the cooked **Chicken Breast** and set aside in a bowl.
3. Add 2 tbsp **Olive Oil** to the pot with the **Onions** and soften until they start to turn translucent.
4. Add the **Red Bell Pepper**, **Chorizo**, and **Cajun Seasoning** and stir for 2–3 minutes.
5. Add the **Rice**, **Chopped Tomatoes**, and **Chicken Stock** and mix well.
6. Return the chicken to the pot. Bring to a simmer, then turn heat to low and cover.
7. Stir occasionally and simmer for 20 minutes, or until the rice is soft.

## Notes

- I like it spicier, so I add Tabasco to my portion.
- My boys like to add southwest sauce for a bit of smoky creaminess.
- My youngest gets sour cream to cool it down.
