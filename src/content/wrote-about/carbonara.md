Not terribly exciting, but very reliable to satisfy the boys.

<!-- excerpt -->

| Serves | Cook Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 20 min |

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amt | Prepped |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dry Pasta | 500g | |
| Onion | 1 large | Diced |
| Garden Peas | 250g | |
| Bacon | 1 pack | Diced |
| Eggs | 4 large | Beaten |
| Sour Cream | 1 small pot | |

## Steps

1. Put **Dry Pasta** on to boil.
2. In a second pot, on medium-high heat, cook the **Onions** and **Bacon** through.
3. Add **Garden Peas** to the second pot and warm through.
4. Once **Pasta** is cooked, strain and add to the second pot.
5. Add **Eggs**, turn heat down to low, and stir through until eggs are cooked.
6. Add **Sour Cream** to the second pot and stir through.
7. Ready to serve.
