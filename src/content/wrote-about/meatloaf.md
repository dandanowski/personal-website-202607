The boys love Grandma's meatloaf and who can blame them.

<!-- excerpt -->

| Serves | Cook Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4–6 | 60 min |

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amt |
| --- | --- |
| Beef Mince | 1kg |
| Italian Bread Crumbs | 1 cup |
| Onion Powder | ½ tbsp |
| Milk | ½ cup |
| Salt and Pepper | To taste |

## Steps

1. Hand mix **Beef Mince**, **Italian Bread Crumbs**, **Onion Powder**, and **Salt and Pepper**.
2. Slowly add **Milk** until the mixture is soft and holds together.
3. Shape into a loaf and place in a baking dish.
4. Bake at 180°C for 45–60 minutes, until no longer pink in the middle.
