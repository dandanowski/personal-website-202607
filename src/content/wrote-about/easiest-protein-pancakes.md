A quick high-protein breakfast. Blend, pour, done.

<!-- excerpt -->

| Serves | Cook Time |
| --- | --- |
| 8 pancakes | 5 min |

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amt |
| --- | --- |
| Rolled Oats | 1 cup + 1 tbsp |
| Baking Powder | 2¼ tsp |
| Protein Powder | ¼ cup |
| Eggs | 3 small |
| Greek Yogurt | ⅓ cup |
| Vanilla Extract | ¼ tsp |
| Maple Syrup | 1 tbsp |
| Coconut Oil | 1 tbsp |

## Steps

1. Blend **Rolled Oats** in a blender until they reach a flour-like texture.
2. Add the rest of the ingredients and blend until smooth.
3. Cook in a pan on medium heat until done on both sides.
