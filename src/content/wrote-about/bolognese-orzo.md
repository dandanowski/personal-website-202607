A quick one-pot dish that comes together in about 30 minutes.

<!-- excerpt -->

| Serves | Cook Time |
| --- | --- |
| 2–3 | 30 min |

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amt |
| --- | --- |
| Beef Mince | 500g |
| Passata | 500g |
| Dry Orzo | 1 cup |
| Water | 1 cup |
| Beef Stock | 1 cube |
| Grated Cheddar | 1 handful |

## Steps

1. Brown the **Beef Mince** with a little oil.
2. Add **Passata**, **Orzo**, **Beef Stock Cube**, and **1 cup water**.
3. Simmer for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent it from sticking.
4. Stir through **Grated Cheddar** and serve.
